About Clinic

Clinic connects people with independent practitioners and gives those practitioners a clinical knowledge assistant that answers only from a curated, graded library of protocols, articles and clinical material — every claim cited back to its source.

Practitioners keep their own client records in a private vault: no client's data is ever reachable from another practitioner's account, and the knowledge library a practitioner queries never contains client-identifying information.

Browse the practitioner directory to find someone to work with, or apply to join as a practitioner if you'd like to offer your own practice through Clinic.
